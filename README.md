## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Hohokum** is an imaginative adventure platformer available for **Windows**, developed by Honeyslug and published by Sony Interactive Entertainment. This unique game encourages players to explore a world filled with colorful minimalistic objects and whimsical NPCs across **17 distinct, unconnected worlds**. The latest version offers a **complete software package** that emphasizes creativity and exploration without the confines of a traditional narrative.

With no strict instructions, players navigate the charming creature known as Long Mover, completing various goals such as waking it up by tickling its eyes. The game fosters an environment of discovery, allowing players to interact freely with their surroundings.
